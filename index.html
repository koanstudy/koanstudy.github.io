Ravn.

Solving the essential so you can focus on the core.

Ravn is a business partnership formed by Judy Watkins and James Holloway to provide bespoke business consulting to new and growing businesses, digital and technology teams and businesses, and to third sector and non-profit causes.

Judy and James first met in the early days of Slack, and collectively share more than five decades’ experience in sectors as diverse as technology, law, finance, security, and government.

Collectively, we connect right- and left- brain analysis, creativity and problem-solving to help businesses and teams meet unique challenges, helping them carry out essential business operations while focusing on their core revenue-generating work.

Ravn deliberately keeps a low profile, working with select clients to build high-trust relationships.

We have helped clients solve a wide variety of problems in and around:

  • Internal communications
  • Business operations
  • Documentation
  • Compliance and accreditation
  • Securing investment
