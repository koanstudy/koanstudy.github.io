Services

Editorial refinement

Developmental editing and rewriting for articles, reports, white papers, and thought leadership. We take your raw draft, transcript, or rough notes and edit them for maximum flow, clarity, tone, and impact. This service covers technical subject matter, heavy narrative lifting, and stylistic precision. We distill. We don’t pad.

Strategic content design

For web copy, UX writing, and digital product flows. Beyond standard editing, this involves structuring information for user needs, accessibility compliance, interaction logic, and content hierarchy.

We can guarantee a Hemingway readability grade of 8 or below if needed.

Discovery bolt-on

Ideal for time-poor clients or those who prefer speaking to writing. This package covers the entire pre-production process:

  • The workshop: A focused briefing call to extract your key ideas.
  • The synthesis: We use AI tools to condense the transcript into a raw draft.
  • The savings: The editing rate is applied only to this reduced word count, lowering the total project cost.

Social amplification

Turn your long-form content into ready-to-post social assets. We offer packages for LinkedIn thought leadership to boost credibility, as well as short-form updates for fast-moving feeds like Bluesky.

Delivery formats

All completed work is available in the format that suits your workflow:

  • Rich Text: Google Doc or Microsoft Word (.docx).
  • Markdown: Clean syntax (.md) ideal for CMS upload or developer hand-off.
  • Plain Text: Unformatted text files (.txt) for maximum compatibility.
